@chick
听|说话人看到了什么？|Look, the little chick is yellow.|一只黄色小鸡 / 一只黄色小鸟 / 一只黄色小鸭 / 一只黄色小猫|chick 是小鸡。
单|The ______ are playing in the garden.|chicks / chick / chickens / chickes|主语是复数，chick 的复数是 chicks。|变形
选|chick、chicken|Look at that ______ over there. It is very small.|chick|指小鸡用 chick；chicken 多指鸡或鸡肉。|易混
形|Two ______ came out of the eggs. (chick)|chicks|two 后面用复数 chicks。|变形
改|The chick is very small and cute.|无 → 无|句子正确，chick 单数作主语，is 搭配。|
完|这只小鸡很可爱。|This ______ is very cute.|chick|小鸡用 chick。|用法
单|There are five ______ on the farm.|chicks / chick / chicken / chickes|five 后面用复数 chicks。|变形
@edge
听|说话人站在哪里？|Don't stand near the edge of the cliff.|悬崖边 / 悬崖顶 / 悬崖下 / 悬崖中间|edge 是边缘。
单|Be careful! The knife has a sharp ______.|edge / border / side / margin|刀刃锋利用 edge。|易混
选|edge、border|The river is the ______ between the two countries.|border|两国之间的边界用 border。|易混
形|He ______ slowly along the wall yesterday. (edge)|edged|yesterday 用过去式 edged。|变形
改|She is edging careful along the narrow bridge.|careful → carefully|修饰动词 edging 用副词 carefully。|
完|他紧张不安地等着结果。|He was on ______ while waiting for the result.|edge|on edge 表示紧张不安。|短语
单|The ______ of the table is very sharp.|edge / border / side / margin|桌子的边线用 edge。|用法
@double-edged
听|说话人怎么形容这把剑？|This sword is double-edged and very dangerous.|双刃的 / 单刃的 / 很长的 / 很旧的|double-edged 是双刃的。
单|A ______ sword can cut both ways.|double-edged / double / edged / edge|双刃的用 double-edged。|用法
选|double-edged、edged|The knife is ______, so be careful.|double-edged|双刃的用 double-edged。|易混
形|This ______ sword is dangerous. (double-edge)|double-edged|复合形容词用 double-edged。|变形
改|This is a double-edge sword.|double-edge → double-edged|复合形容词要加 -ed。|变形
完|这把剑是双刃的。|This sword is ______.|double-edged|双刃的用 double-edged。|用法
单|The word “double-edged” means ______.|双刃的 / 单刃的 / 无刃的 / 钝的|double-edged 意为双刃的。|用法
