@buzz
听|说话人问的是什么？|Can you give me a buzz tonight?|今晚给我打个电话 / 今晚来我家 / 今晚发条消息 / 今晚按门铃|give sb. a buzz 给某人打电话。
单|The bees ______ around the flowers now.|are buzzing / buzzes / buzzed / buzz|now 表示现在进行，用 are buzzing。|
选|buzz、drone|The phone ______ woke me up at six.|buzzed|手机“嗡嗡”响用 buzz；drone 指机器低沉的嗡嗡声。|
形|The door ______ when you press the button. (buzz)|buzzes|主语 the door 是第三人称单数，buzz 加 es。|变形
改|The bee buzzs loudly in the garden.|buzzs → buzzes|buzz 三单加 es，不直接加 s。|
完|他昨天按蜂鸣器叫了门卫。|He ______ the buzzer for the guard yesterday.|buzzed|yesterday 表示过去，用过去式 buzzed。|变形
单|There is a ______ about a new park in town.|buzz / buzzing / buzzed / buzzes|there is 后接名词，传闻用 buzz。|词性转换
@buzzer
听|说话人说什么响了？|The buzzer rang, so I opened the door.|蜂鸣器 / 电话 / 闹钟 / 门铃|buzzer 是“嗡嗡”的蜂鸣器。
单|Please press the ______ to call the nurse.|buzzer / bell / alarm / buzz|按蜂鸣器叫人用 buzzer；bell 是叮铃的铃。|易混
选|buzzer、bell|The school ______ rings at eight every morning.|bell|学校“叮铃”的铃是 bell，不是 buzzer。|易混
形|There are two ______ on the wall. (buzzer)|buzzers|two 后面用复数 buzzers。|变形
改|The buzzer ring loudly and everyone heard it.|ring → rang|and 后 heard 是过去式，前面也用过去式 rang。|
完|门口的蜂鸣器坏了。|The ______ at the door is broken.|buzzer|门口“嗡嗡”响的装置是 buzzer。|易混
单|The ______ went off and we all left the building.|alarm / buzzer / bell / buzz|火警警报用 alarm，不是蜂鸣器。|易混
@victor
听|说话人说的是谁？|The victor raised the cup and smiled.|获胜者 / 裁判 / 教练 / 观众|victor 是胜利者、获胜者。
单|The ______ of the race got a gold medal.|victor / winner / conqueror / victory|赛跑获胜者用 victor 或 winner；这里考 victor 拼写。|易混
选|victor、conqueror|The ______ took the city and ruled it for years.|conqueror|带“征服、占领”意味用 conqueror。|易混
形|The two ______ shook hands after the game. (victor)|victors|two 后面用复数 victors。|变形
改|The victor were very happy after the match.|were → was|the victor 是单数，be 动词用 was。|
完|他是这场比赛的胜利者。|He is the ______ of this match.|victor|比赛的胜者用 victor。|易混
单|Every ______ in the game gets a small prize.|winner / victor / conqueror / victory|一般的“获胜者”用 winner；victor 偏书面。|易混
