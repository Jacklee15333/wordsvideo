@ sausage
听|说话人早餐想吃什么？|I'd like two sausages for breakfast.|两根香肠 / 两个鸡蛋 / 两片面包 / 两杯牛奶|two sausages 两根香肠。
单|There ______ some sausages on the plate.|are / is / has / have|sausages 是复数，there be 用 are。|
选|sausage、salami|I'd like a piece of ______, the dry Italian one.|salami|salami 是意式风干腌肠；sausage 是统称。|
形|My mother bought three ______ this morning. (sausage)|sausages|three 后面用复数 sausages。|
改|I ate two sausage for lunch yesterday.|sausage → sausages|two 后面可数名词用复数。|
完|我想要一个热狗，请给我一个夹香肠的面包。|I'd like a hot dog, please give me a bread ______ a sausage in it.|with|with 表示“带有”，夹着香肠。|短语
单|The ______ in this shop are fresh and cheap.|sausages / sausage / salami / salsa|主语是复数，用 sausages；salami 不可数，salsa 是酱。|易混
@ salsa
听|说话人喜欢跳什么舞？|My sister loves dancing salsa very much.|萨尔萨舞 / 芭蕾舞 / 踢踏舞 / 街舞|salsa 萨尔萨舞。
单|We need some ______ for the chips, a spicy tomato sauce.|salsa / sauce / sausage / dust|salsa 指拉美风味辣酱；sauce 是一般的酱汁。|
选|salsa、sauce|This dance comes from Latin America and is called ______.|salsa|这种拉美舞叫 salsa；sauce 是酱汁。|
形|They are learning two ______ this term. (salsa)|salsas|two 后面用复数 salsas（指两种萨尔萨舞曲）。|
改|I like to eat chips with tomato salsa, it is a kind of dance.|dance → sauce|tomato sauce 番茄酱；salsa 是舞或辣酱，这里说吃的酱用 sauce。|
完|我姐姐每周都跳萨尔萨舞。|My sister dances ______ every week.|salsa|dances salsa 跳萨尔萨舞。|短语
单|There are many kinds of ______ in the Latin dance class.|salsas / salsa / sauce / sauces|many kinds of 后面用复数 salsas。|变形
@ dust
听|说话人说什么东西到处都是？|There is dust all over the old books.|灰尘 / 水 / 墨水 / 油|dust 灰尘。
单|The old table is covered with ______.|dust / dusts / a dust / dusty|dust 不可数，没有复数，也不加 a。|
选|dust、dirt|Please clean the ______ off the windows; it's grey and dry.|dust|窗户上灰蒙蒙的干灰用 dust；dirt 偏污垢。|
形|After the storm, the road was very ______. (dust)|dusty|be 后面用形容词 dusty（满是灰尘的）。|
改|She dusted the cake with sugar yesterday.|dusted → dusted|dust 作“撒”讲，过去式 dusted 正确。|
完|他每天打扫书架上的灰尘。|He cleans the ______ off the bookshelf every day.|dust|灰尘用不可数名词 dust。|用法
单|The farmer ______ the crops with powder last week.|dusted / dusts / dusting / dust|last week 用过去式 dusted。|变形
