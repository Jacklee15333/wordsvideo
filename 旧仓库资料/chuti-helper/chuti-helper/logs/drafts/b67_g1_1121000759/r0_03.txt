@virtually
听|说话人说的这座城市怎么样？|This city is virtually the same as my hometown.|几乎一样 / 完全不同 / 大得多 / 更漂亮|virtually 意思是“几乎、差不多”。
单|The two pictures are ______ the same. I can't tell them apart.|virtually / virtual / virtue / nearly|修饰 the same 表示“几乎”用 virtually。|易混
选|virtually、almost|The work is ______ finished, so we can go home now.|almost|almost 不能直接修饰 the same 这类词，此处用 almost 更自然；virtually 也可，但本题考常用搭配。|易混
形|We had a ______ meeting last week. (virtually)|virtual|修饰名词 meeting 要用形容词 virtual（线上的）。|词性转换
改|He virtually finished the work, but he still had one page to do.|virtually → almost|句意是“几乎完成但还差一页”，用 almost 更准确；virtually 强调“实际上差不多”。|易混
完|他几乎每天都早起。|He gets up early ______ every day.|virtually|“几乎每天”用 virtually。|用法
单|Her ______ is known to all her friends.|virtue / virtual / virtually / virtues|物主代词后接名词，此处指“美德”用 virtue。|词性转换
