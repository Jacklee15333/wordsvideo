@slip
听|说话人昨天怎么了？|I slipped on the wet floor yesterday.|滑倒了 / 摔伤了腿 / 丢了钥匙 / 迟到了|slip on the wet floor 在湿地板上滑倒。
单|Be careful, or you will ______ on the ice.|slip / slide / sleep / stop|在冰上意外滑倒用 slip；slide 是平稳滑行。|易混
选|slip、slide|The children like to ______ down the hill on the snow.|slide|slide down 在表面上平稳地滑行；slip 是意外滑倒。|易混
形|He ______ on the wet road last night. (slip)|slipped|过去式双写 p 加 ed：slipped。|变形
改|She slips on the banana skin and fell down.|slips → slipped|and 后 fell 是过去式，前面也用过去式 slipped。|变形
完|他正小心翼翼地走在结冰的路上，以免再次滑倒。|He is walking carefully on the icy road to avoid ______ again.|slipping|avoid 后接动名词，slip 双写 p 加 ing。|变形
单|Don't ______ up on such a small thing.|slip / slide / sleep / stop|slip up 是固定短语，意为“疏忽出错”。|短语
@slippery
听|说话人说路怎么样？|The road is very slippery after the rain.|很滑 / 很宽 / 很脏 / 很挤|slippery 意为“滑的”。
单|Be careful! The floor is ______ after the rain.|slippery / slip / slipper / slipped|be 后接形容词 slippery（滑的）。|词性转换
选|slippery、slip|The wet floor is very ______, so walk slowly.|slippery|系动词 is 后要用形容词 slippery。|词性转换
形|The fish is too ______ to hold in my hand. (slip)|slippery|too … to 结构中用形容词 slippery（滑的）。|词性转换
改|The stone is very slipperyly after the rain.|slipperyly → slippery|be 后接形容词，不用副词。|词性转换
完|这条路又湿又滑，我们慢点走。|The road is wet and ______, so let's walk slowly.|slippery|形容词作表语，意为“滑的”。|词性转换
单|Learning to lie is a ______ slope to bad habits.|slippery / slip / slipper / slipped|slippery slope 是固定搭配，比喻“滑坡”。|短语
