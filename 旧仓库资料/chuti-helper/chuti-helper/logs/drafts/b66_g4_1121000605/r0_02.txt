听|说话人觉得那个人怎么样？|The man was too crafty to trust.|太狡猾，不能信 / 很诚实，可以信 / 很懒，不干活 / 很忙，没时间|crafty 狡猾的，too crafty to trust 太狡猾不能信。
单|The fox in the story is very ______.|crafty / craft / craftily / crafts|作表语说“狡猾的”用形容词 crafty。|
选|crafty、cunning|He is a ______ old man who always plans ahead.|cunning|crafty 重手法巧妙、老谋深算；这里说会算计、会钻空子用 cunning。|
形|This old fox is even ______ than the one in the story. (crafty)|craftier|even 后面用比较级，crafty 变 y 为 i 加 er。|
改|The crafty fox tricked the crow and got the meat.|crafty → crafty|crafty 拼写正确，y 前是辅音字母，比较级才变 i。|
完|这个狡猾的商人骗了所有人。|The ______ businessman fooled everyone.|crafty|修饰名词“商人”用形容词 crafty。|用法
单|He is good with his hands and makes beautiful chairs. We say he is ______.|skilful / crafty / sly / foxy|skilful|夸人手巧用 skilful，crafty 是贬义“狡猾的”。|易混

单|The ______ of this watch is excellent.|craftsmanship / craftsman / crafty / craftsmen|craftsmanship|the … of 后面要名词 craftsmanship（做工）。|

改|This old bridge shows the great craftsmanship of the workers.|craftsmanship → craftsmanship|craftsmanship 不可数，没有复数，这里用法正确。|
完|这把椅子的做工非常好。|The ______ of this chair is very good.|craftsmanship|“做工”用不可数名词 craftsmanship。|词性转换
单|We all admire the ______ of these hand-made bags.|craftsmanship / craftsman / craft / crafty|craftsmanship|the … of 结构要名词 craftsmanship（工艺水准）。|词性转换
