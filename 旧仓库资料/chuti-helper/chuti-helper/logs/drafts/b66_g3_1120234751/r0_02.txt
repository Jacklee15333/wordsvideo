改|My grandma grows many herb in her garden.|herb → herbs|many 后面接复数名词 herbs。|

完|她用草药治好了那个男孩。|She cured the boy with ______.|herbs|介词 with 后面接名词，泛指用复数 herbs。|
选|herb、herbal|My aunt drinks ______ tea every morning.|herbal|tea 前面要用形容词 herbal；herb 是名词。|词性转换

@herbalist
听|说话人的叔叔是做什么的？|My uncle is a herbalist and he helps sick people with plants.|草药医生 / 牙医 / 护士 / 司机|herbalist 是草药医生。
单|______ use plants to cure people.|Herbalists / Herbalist / Herbal / Herbally|主语是复数，用 Herbalists。|
改|Two herbalist work in the small shop.|herbalist → herbalists|Two 后面要用复数 herbalists。|
形|She is one of the best ______ (herbalist) here.|herbalists|one of the best 后面用复数名词 herbalists。|变形
完|这位草药医生住在我们村里。|This ______ lives in our village.|herbalist|单数主语与 lives 一致，用 herbalist。|
选|herbal、herbalist
