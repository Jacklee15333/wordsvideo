@toughness
听|说话人说这种钢怎么样？|This kind of steel is famous for its toughness.|以韧性闻名 / 以硬度闻名 / 以重量闻名 / 以价格闻名|be famous for its toughness 以韧性好而闻名。
单|The ______ of this new material surprised us all.|toughness / tough / toughly / toughen|the 与 of 之间需要名词 toughness（韧性）。|
形|He is a ______ boy and never gives up. (toughness)|tough|修饰名词 boy 要用形容词 tough。|
选|tough、toughly|The meat was too ______ for the old man.|tough|系动词 was 后作表语要用形容词 tough，toughly 是副词。|
改|This box is made of a very toughness plastic.|toughness → tough|plastic 是名词，前面要用形容词 tough 修饰。|
完|我们老师说这种纸没有多少韧性。|Our teacher says this paper doesn't have ______ toughness.|much|toughness 是不可数名词，要用 much 修饰（不能用 many）。|变形
单|Glass is very ______, but it is not tough at all.|hard / tough / hardness / toughly|玻璃很硬却不韧；be 后作表语用形容词 hard，hardness 是名词。|易混
@tough-looking
听|说话人觉得门口那个高个子男人怎么样？|The tall man at the door is tough-looking.|看着不好惹 / 很友好 / 很年轻 / 很累|tough-looking 意思是“看着凶悍的、不好惹的”。
单|The tall man over there is a ______ guy.|tough-looking / tough-look / tough-looked / toughly-looking|复合形容词由“形容词 + -looking”构成，用来修饰名词 guy。|
改|He is a tough-look man, but he is kind.|tough-look → tough-looking|-looking 结尾的复合形容词要用连字符连接，不能写成 tough-look。|
形|The meat was too ______ for the little boy. (tough)|tough|tough 表示食物“老、嚼不动”，在 be 后作表语。|
完|那个穿黑衣服的男人看着不好惹。|The man in black ______ ______.|looks；tough|look 是系动词，后接形容词 tough；主语是单数，用 looks。|
选|tough、tough-looking|This old rope is very ______ and hard to break.|tough|说东西结实、难断用 tough；tough-looking 只形容人“看着凶悍”。|易混
单|He looks ______, but he is really kind.|tough / toughly / toughness / tough-looking|系动词 look 后接形容词 tough 作表语，tough-looking 不用在 look 后面。|用法
@carrot
听|说话人晚饭需要什么？|We need some carrots for dinner.|胡萝卜 / 土豆 / 面包 / 牛奶|carrots 胡萝卜。
单|There ______ a carrot on the table.|is / are / have / be|主语 a carrot 是单数，there be 句型用 is。|
形|She bought two kilos of ______. (carrot)|carrots|两公斤胡萝卜，可数名词要用复数 carrots。|
改|There are many carrot in the box.|carrot → carrots|many 后面接可数名词复数 carrots。|
完|这根胡萝卜又大又甜。|This ______ is big and sweet.|carrot|指示词 This 后接可数名词单数 carrot。|
选|carrot、carrots|The farmer grows ______ in his field.|carrots|泛指种胡萝卜，可数名词要用复数 carrots。|变形
单|I need ______ carrot for the soup.|a / an / two / many|carrot 是可数名词单数，辅音音素开头用 a；two、many 后面要接复数 carrots。|用法
