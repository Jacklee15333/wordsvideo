@panel
听|这个评审团由几名教师组成？|The panel is made up of five teachers.|五名 / 三名 / 四名 / 六名|made up of five teachers 指五名教师。
单|The ______ on the wall shows the temperature of the room.|panel / piece / pair / page|墙上显示温度的是一块面板，用 panel。|
改|This machine has three panel on the front.|panel → panels|three 后面要用复数 panels。|
选|panel、piece|She gave me a ______ of paper to write on.|piece|paper 不可数，用 a piece of；panel 指面板或小组。|
完|这个评审团由五名教师组成。|The panel ______ ______ of five teachers.|is；made|be made up of 表示“由……组成”。|
形|There are two small ______ on the front of the radio. (panel)|panels|two 后面用复数，panel 直接加 s。|变形
单|A panel of experts ______ coming to our school next Monday.|is / are / were / be|a panel of experts 指一个小组，谓语用单数 is；next Monday 表将来。|短语
@cave

单|Bats sleep in a dark ______ during the day.|cave / caves / cave's / caving|蝙蝠白天睡在黑暗的山洞里，用单数 cave。|
改|The caves in this mountain is very large.|is → are|主语 caves 是复数，谓语要用 are。|
选|cave、hole|There is a small ______ in my sock.|hole|袜子上的小孔用 hole；cave 是天然的大山洞。|
完|这座山洞是许多蝙蝠的家。|This cave is ______ ______ many bats.|home；to|be home to 表示“是……的家”。|
形|We visited two large ______ in Guilin last summer. (cave)|caves|two 后面用复数，cave 直接加 s。|变形
单|The roof of the old house ______ under the heavy snow last night.|caved in / cave in / caves in / caving in|last night 表过去，cave in（塌陷）用过去式 caved in。|短语
@excavate
听|他们去年在河边做了什么？|Last year they excavated an old village near the river.|发掘了一个古村落 / 建了一座新桥 / 参观了一所学校 / 种了很多树|excavated an old village 指发掘古村落。
单|They ______ the old city last summer and found many bowls.|excavated / excavate / excavates / excavating|last summer 表过去，用过去式 excavated。|
改|The team excavate the site every summer.|excavate → excavates|主语 team 是单数，一般现在时用三单 excavates。|
选|excavate、dig|The children like to ______ in the sand on the beach.|dig|日常“挖”用 dig；excavate 是专业的成规模挖掘。|
完|他们现在正在发掘一座古墓。|They are ______ an old tomb now.|excavating|now 表现在进行时，excavate 去 e 加 ing。|
形|The ______ of the old city took many years. (excavate)|excavation|the … of 结构需要名词，excavate 去 e 加 ion 变成 excavation。|词性转换
单|The workers ______ a deep hole for the new pool last week.|dug / excavate / excavates / excavating|日常“挖”用 dig，过去式是 dug；excavate 多用于考古和工程。|易混
