单|There are many fruit ______ in this market.|stalls / stall / stalling / stalled|many 后面接复数 stalls（摊位）。|
改|Our car stall on the way home yesterday.|stall → stalled|yesterday 说明是过去，动词用过去式 stalled。|
选|stall、desk|He bought apples at a fruit ______ in the market.|stall|市场里卖水果的摊位叫 stall，desk 是书桌。|
形|The engine is ______ again. (stall)|stalling|be 后面用现在分词 stalling，表示正在熄火。|
完|这辆公交车经常在半路抛锚。|This bus often ______ on the way.|stalls|often 表示经常，主语单数用三单 stalls。|变形
单|The car ______ on the road, so we had to walk home.|stalled / stalls / stalling / stall|had to 说明事情发生在过去，用过去式 stalled。|用法
