@fridge
听|问：说话人把牛奶放在哪里？|I put the milk in the fridge.|冰箱 / 桌子上 / 书包里 / 汽车里|in the fridge 是在冰箱里。
单|Fridge is short for ______.|refrigerator / freezer / cupboard / cold|fridge 是 refrigerator 的缩写，两者都指冰箱。|易混
改|Mum keeps the eggs on the fridge.|on → in|东西放在冰箱里用 in the fridge。|用法
选|fridge、cupboard|The milk is in the ______, so it stays cold.|fridge|牛奶要冷藏，放 fridge；cupboard 是橱柜，不能保鲜。|易混
形|There are three ______ in the new shop. (fridge)|fridges|以 e 结尾直接加 s，复数 fridges。|变形
完|冰箱能让食物保持低温。|A fridge ______ food cold.|keeps|主语 A fridge 是单数，一般现在时用 keeps。|用法
单|There ______ some milk in the fridge.|is / are / be / am|milk 不可数，there be 句型用 is。|用法
@refrigeration
听|问：什么能让我们的食物保鲜？|Refrigeration keeps our food fresh.|制冷 / 烹饪 / 种植 / 清洁|refrigeration 是制冷、冷藏。
单|A ______ keeps food cold in summer.|refrigerator / refrigeration / refrigerate / refrigerators|指那台机器要用可数名词 refrigerator；refrigeration 是不可数的过程。|易混
改|Modern refrigeration make food stay fresh longer.|make → makes|refrigeration 不可数，作主语动词用三单 makes。|用法
选|refrigeration、refrigerator|The ______ in our kitchen is new.|refrigerator|厨房里那台机器是可数的 refrigerator；refrigeration 指制冷技术。|易混
形|Modern ______ keeps food fresh longer. (refrigerate)|refrigeration|主语位置需要名词，refrigerate 的名词形式是不可数名词 refrigeration。|词性转换
完|这座老房子没有任何制冷设备。|This old house doesn't have ______.|any refrigeration|否定句中不可数名词前用 any，不加 a 也不加 s。|变形
单|There are two ______ in our kitchen.|refrigerators / refrigerator / refrigeration / refrigerations|两台冰箱是可数名词复数 refrigerators；refrigeration 不可数。|易混
@herb
听|问：说话人在花园里种什么？|I grow herbs in my garden.|香草 / 苹果 / 树 / 鱼|herbs 是香草、药草。
单|______ are small plants used in cooking.|Herbs / Herb / Herbes / A herb|作主语的复数用 Herbs，herb 复数直接加 s。|变形
改|This herb smell very nice.|smell → smells|主语 this herb 是单数，动词用 smells。|用法
选|herbs、grass|We need some fresh ______ for the soup.|herbs|做菜用的香草是 herbs；grass 指青草，不能调味。|易混
形|My mother grows many ______ in the garden. (herb)|herbs|many 后面接可数名词复数 herbs。|变形
完|她在汤里加了一些香草。|She added some ______ to the soup.|herbs|add sth to sth 把某物加进某物；some 后接复数 herbs。|短语
单|My grandmother drinks ______ tea every morning.|herb / herbs / herb's / herbes|名词作定语修饰 tea 用单数 herb，herb tea 是花草茶。|用法
