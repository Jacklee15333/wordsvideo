听|说话人说了什么？|The excavation of the old city took three years.|古城的发掘花了三年 / 古城的建造花了三年 / 古城的参观花了三年 / 古城的清洁花了三年|excavation 意思是“发掘”，took three years 表示花了三年。
单|The ______ of the ancient city took ten years.|excavation / excavations / excavate / excavator|主语是单数，of 前用名词单数 excavation（发掘）。|
改|The scientist found many ancient excavation last year.|excavation → excavations|many 后接可数名词复数，用 excavations。|
选|excavation、digging|The children are ______ a hole in the sand now.|digging|现在进行时需要动词 digging；excavation 是名词，不能直接接宾语。|
完|这座古城的发掘花了三年时间。|The excavation of the old city ______ three years.|took|take 表示“花费（时间）”，主语是单数，且事情发生在过去，用 took。|
形|The team found three ______ in the old city last year. (excavation)|excavations|three 后接可数名词复数，excavation 的复数是 excavations。|变形
单|The workers ______ the old city last year.|excavated / excavation / excavations / excavating|句子需要动词作谓语，last year 表示过去，用过去式 excavated。|词性转换
