@cage
听|说话人的鸟住在哪里？|My bird lives in a small cage.|笼子里 / 盒子里 / 花园里 / 树上|cage 是笼子。
单|The police put the thief in ______ for a week.|jail / cage / jails / jailing|关人的地方用 jail；cage 是鸟笼。|
形|The shop sells three ______ for birds. (cage)|cages|three 后面接复数 cages。|
改|The zookeeper is cageing the new lion now.|cageing → caging|cage 去 e 加 ing，写成 caging。|
完|昨天他们把鸟关进了笼子。|They ______ the bird yesterday.|caged|yesterday 说明用过去式，cage 去 e 加 d 变 caged。|
单|My grandpa ______ his birds every morning.|cages / cage / caging / caged|every morning 表习惯，主语三单，用 cages。|变形
选|jail、cage|The bird is singing in its ______.|cage|鸟住的是 cage；jail 关人。|易混

完|小鸟在笼子里唱歌。|The little bird is singing in a ______.|cage|in a cage 表示在笼子里。|
