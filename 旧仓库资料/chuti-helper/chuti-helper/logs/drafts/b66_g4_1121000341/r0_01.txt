@单词：@gasp
听|她看到大蛇时做了什么？|She gasped when she saw the big snake.|倒吸一口气 / 大声唱歌 / 笑了起来 / 转身跑开|gasped 是吃惊时突然吸一口气。
单|She ______ in surprise when she heard the news.|gasped / panted / sighed / slept|吃惊时突然吸一口气用 gasp；pant 是跑步后一口接一口地喘，sigh 是叹气。|
改|She gasp at the loud noise yesterday.|gasp → gasped|yesterday 是过去时间，动词用过去式 gasped。|
形|The old man was still ______ after running fast. (gasp)|gasping|was 后面用现在分词，构成过去进行时 gasping。|
完|他喘着气说出了这个消息。|He ______ out the news.|gasped|gasp out 表示喘着气说。|短语
选|gasp、sigh|She ______ with relief when the exam ended.|sighed|sigh 是长长呼出一口气、叹气；gasp 是突然吸气。|易混
单|He often ______ when he sees a snake.|gasps / gasp / gasping / gasped|often 表示一般现在时，主语 He 用三单 gasps。|变形
@lane
听|说话人住在什么样的地方？|I live on a quiet lane near the park.|安静的小巷 / 繁忙的大街 / 大广场 / 河边小路|lane 是小路、小巷，quiet lane 是安静的小巷。
单|The cyclist stayed in his ______ on the busy road.|lane / alley / path / street|马路上划出的车道用 lane；alley 是楼房之间的小巷。|
改|There are two narrow lane near my house.|lane → lanes|two 后面要用复数 lanes。|
选|path、lane|We walked along a narrow ______ across the field to the river.|path|田野里走出来的小径用 path；lane 是两边有界的窄路。|
完|开车时你应该待在自己的车道里。|When you drive, you should ______.|stay in your lane|stay in your lane 表示待在自己的车道里，别越界。|短语
形|There are three ______ on this wide road. (lane)|lanes|three 后面用复数 lanes。|变形
改|Small lanes lie between the tall buildings, so we often get lost.|lanes → alleys|楼房之间的小巷用 alley；lane 是两边有界的窄路。|易混
@craft
听|奶奶擅长什么？|My grandma is good at the craft of making paper flowers.|做纸花的手艺 / 种花 / 画画 / 做蛋糕|the craft of making paper flowers 是做纸花的手艺。
单|The astronauts flew a small ______ to the moon.|craft / boat / car / train|craft 可指船或飞行器，单复数同形。|
改|He craft beautiful toys every day.|craft → crafts|every day 表示一般现在时，主语 He 用三单 crafts。|
选|craft、wrist|She hurt her ______ when she fell off the bike.|wrist|手腕是 wrist；craft 是手艺、工艺。|
形|The shop sells many beautiful ______. (craft)|crafts|many 后面用可数名词复数 crafts。|
完|她用木头精心制作了一只小船。|She ______ a small boat out of wood.|crafted|过去做的事用过去式 crafted。|变形
单|Look! The girl ______ a small boat out of wood.|is crafting / crafts / crafted / craft|Look! 表示现在进行时，用 is crafting。|变形
