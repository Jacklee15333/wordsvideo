@theoretically
听|说话人觉得这个计划怎么样？|Theoretically, this plan should work well.|理论上应该可行 / 一定能成功 / 完全行不通 / 已经失败过|theoretically 意为“理论上”。
单|We need a ______ explanation of how it works.|theoretical / theoretically / theory / theories|名词 explanation 前用形容词 theoretical。|
改|Theoretical, the new rule should help students a lot.|Theoretical → Theoretically|句首作状语修饰全句，要用副词 Theoretically。|
完|理论上，这个计划会成功。|In ______, this plan will work.|theory|in theory 意为“理论上”，与 theoretically 同义。|
形|______, the two cities are the same size. (theoretical)|Theoretically|句首作状语要用副词 Theoretically。|
单|Theoretically, our team should win, ______ we often lose.|but / so / because / or|theoretically 只说“理论上”，常暗示实际未必如此，后用 but 转折。|用法
选|practice、theory|Theoretically the plan is perfect, but in ______ it is hard.|practice|in practice 意为“实际上”，与 theoretically 相对。|短语
@recover
听|医生说什么？|He will recover from the illness soon.|他很快会康复 / 他病得更重了 / 他需要做手术 / 他已经出院了|recover from 意为“从……中康复”。
单|She ______ from her bad cold last week.|recovered / recovers / recovering / recover|last week 表示过去，用过去式 recovered。|
形|Now he is slowly ______ from the accident. (recover)|recovering|now 和 is 表示现在进行时，用 recovering。|
改|Scientists recover a new star last year.|recover → discovered|“发现”新事物用 discover 的过去式；recover 表示“找回、康复”。|
完|昨天他找回了丢失的钱包。|He ______ his lost wallet yesterday.|recovered|yesterday 表示过去，recover 意为“找回”，用过去式 recovered。|
单|His ______ after the operation was slow.|recovery / recover / recovered / recovering|物主代词 his 后要用名词 recovery。|词性转换
选|from、of|It took her a month to recover ______ the illness.|from|recover from 是固定搭配，意为“从……中康复”。|短语
@recovery
听|说话人说什么需要很长时间？|The patient's full recovery will take a long time.|完全康复 / 一次手术 / 一次检查 / 出院手续|recovery 意为“康复、恢复”。
单|Doctors helped him ______ from the illness.|recover / recovers / recovery / recovered|help sb. do sth. 后用动词原形 recover。|
改|The recovery of the old temple took ten years.|recovery → restoration|修复建筑、文物用 restoration；recovery 多指康复或经济复苏。|
完|他上个月手术之后康复得很快。|He ______ quickly after the operation last month.|recovered|last month 表示过去，用过去式 recovered。|
选|from、of|His recovery ______ the accident took two months.|from|recovery from 意为“从……中康复”。|
形|Doctors have seen many quick ______ this year. (recovery)|recoveries|many 后接可数名词复数，recovery 变 recoveries。|变形
单|The ______ of the stolen money made the old man happy.|recovery / recover / recovering / recovered|这里 recovery 表示“找回、追回”这个动作。|用法
#问题 recover：同义词里列了 reintroduce，它不是“恢复”的同义词，reintroduce 意为“重新引入”，应删去。
