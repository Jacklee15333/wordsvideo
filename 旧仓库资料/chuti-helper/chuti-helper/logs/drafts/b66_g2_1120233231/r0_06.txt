@username
听|说话人要你在这里输入什么？|Please type your username in this box.|用户名 / 真名 / 密码 / 邮箱|username 是登录用的用户名。
单|Your ______ is the name you use to log in, not your real name.|username / name / nickname / number|登录用的标识叫 username，真名是 name。|易混
改|Your username are too long for this box.|are → is|主语 username 是单数，be 动词用 is。|用法
选|username、name|Please write your real ______ on the form.|name|表格上写真名用 name；username 是登录名。|易混
形|This computer has three ______ on it. (username)|usernames|three 后面用复数 usernames。|变形
完|你可以用用户名和密码登录。|You can log in ______ your username and password.|with|用某物用介词 with，log in with … 是常用搭配。|短语
单|How many ______ does this game have?|usernames / username / username's / usernaming|How many 后面接复数名词 usernames。|变形
@nickname
听|说话人想知道什么？|What's your nickname at school?|外号是什么 / 真名是什么 / 家在哪里 / 几岁了|nickname 是外号、昵称。
单|We call him Bob, but Bob is only his ______.|nickname / name / username / number|大家叫的那个名字是 nickname（昵称、外号）。|易混
形|Last year the students ______ their teacher “King”. (nickname)|nicknamed|last year 是过去时间，动词用过去式 nicknamed（去 e 加 d）。|变形
改|My nickname are hard to remember.|are → is|主语 nickname 是单数，be 动词用 is。|
选|nickname、name|Please write your real ______ on the form, not your nickname.|name|表格上写真名 name；nickname 是外号。|
完|现在在学校他被大家叫做“猫”。|These days he ______ ______ “Cat” at school.|is；nicknamed|be nicknamed 被叫做，用被动语态；these days 表示现在，用 is。|短语
单|My two best friends have funny ______.|nicknames / nickname / nicknaming / nickname's|two 后面接复数名词 nicknames。|变形
@anonymous
听|说话人说什么样的人捐了钱？|An anonymous person gave the school a lot of money.|一个匿名的人 / 一位老师 / 一名学生 / 校长的朋友|anonymous 意为“匿名的”。
单|The letter was sent ______, so nobody knows the writer.|anonymously / anonymous / confidential / secret|修饰动词 was sent 要用副词 anonymously。|
完|医生对我的个人信息保密。|The doctor kept my information ______.|confidential|知道是谁但不外传用 confidential。|
选|anonymous、confidential|Nobody knows who wrote this letter. The writer is ______.|anonymous|没人知道作者是谁，用 anonymous（匿名的）。|
改|An anonymous letter were sent to the school.|were → was|主语 letter 是单数，be 动词用 was。|
形|The money was given to the school ______. (anonymous)|anonymously|修饰动词 was given 用副词 anonymously。|词性转换
单|Please keep this report ______; only the manager may read it.|confidential / anonymous / public / famous|知道是谁但不外传用 confidential；anonymous 是不知道是谁。|易混
