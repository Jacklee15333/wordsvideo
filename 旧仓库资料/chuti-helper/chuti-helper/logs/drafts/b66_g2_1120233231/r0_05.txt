@renowned
听|说话人说什么很出名？|The restaurant is renowned for its fish.|那家餐馆的鱼 / 那家餐馆的价格 / 那家餐馆的位置 / 那家餐馆的服务|be renowned for 是“因……而闻名”，句中说的是鱼。
单|The city is renowned ______ its beautiful gardens.|for / as / with / of|be renowned for 是固定搭配，表示“因……而闻名”。|短语
改|The coach is renowned for train young players.|train → training|for 是介词，后面要接动名词 training。|用法
选|renowned、notorious|The man is ______ for cheating old people.|notorious|renowned 是褒义“享有盛誉的”，坏人做坏事要用贬义的 notorious。|易混
完|这位歌手作为一名舞蹈家而享有盛誉。|The singer is renowned ______ a dancer.|as|表示“作为……而闻名”用 be renowned as。|短语
形|The ______ doctor has saved many lives. (renown)|renowned|renown 是名词，修饰名词 doctor 要用形容词 renowned。|词性转换
单|This garden is ______ than that one.|more renowned / renownedest / most renowned / renowned|than 前面要用比较级；renowned 是多音节词，比较级加 more。|变形
@name
听|说话人请对方做什么？|Please write your name on the paper.|在纸上写名字 / 在纸上画图 / 大声读课文 / 交作业|write your name on the paper 表示在纸上写名字。
单|In "The old shop has a good name", "a good name" means ______.|好名声 / 好名字 / 好朋友 / 好地方|a good name 指“好名声”，不是“好听的名字”。|用法
选|name、fame|The young actor is looking for ______ and money.|fame|fame 指“名声”，name 主要指“名字”。|易混
改|My name are Lily.|are → is|主语 name 是单数，be 动词要用 is。|用法
形|The ______ of the two cities are the same. (name)|names|two cities 说明是复数，name 要用复数 names。|变形
完|昨天他们用我爷爷的名字给这条街命名。|Yesterday they ______ the street after my grandpa.|named|句中有 yesterday，要用过去式 named；name ... after 是“以……命名”。|短语
单|The teacher will ______ the winner tomorrow.|name / names / named / naming|will 后面用动词原形；这里 name 作动词，意思是“宣布、任命”。|词性转换
@rename
听|他们去年对学校做了什么？|They renamed the school last year.|给学校改了名 / 建了一所新学校 / 参观了学校 / 关掉了学校|renamed 是“重新命名、改名”。
单|The street ______ after the hero last year.|was renamed / renamed / renames / is renaming|街道是“被命名”的，last year 说明用过去分词构成的被动语态。|用法
改|The city renameed the street last year.|renameed → renamed|rename 以 e 结尾，过去式去掉 e 再加 d。|变形
选|rename、name|They will ______ the new library after the writer.|name|给新图书馆取名用 name；rename 是把已有的名字换掉。|易混
形|They are ______ the little park now. (rename)|renaming|now 表示正在进行，去掉 e 加 ing 变 renaming。|变形
完|请不要给这个文件夹改名。|Please don't ______ the folder.|rename|don't 后面接动词原形 rename（重新命名）。|用法
单|The word "rename" means to give something ______ name.|a new / an old / no / the same|re- 表示“再一次”，所以 rename 是“给……一个新名字”。|用法
